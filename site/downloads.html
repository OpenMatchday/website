Downloads

Source is public under MIT on GitHub. Build from the repositories below. This page does not list store packages, purchase links, or unsigned binaries that have not been published by a release task.

Source repositories

  • OpenMatchday/app

    Shared application, role shells, and event management.

  • OpenMatchday/media

    Capture, encode, transport, decode, compositing, recording, streaming.

  • OpenMatchday/docs

    Operator, developer, and contributor documentation, including spikes.

  • All OpenMatchday repositories

    Protocol, sports, graphics, themes, social, teams, community, design, website, and org workflows.

Release status

Tagged product releases with checksummed artifacts are not claimed on this page until the release task publishes them. Prefer building from tagged commits once they exist; until then use the default branch and recorded CI status on each repository.

Local-first. Running a match does not require an Open Matchday account or Internet access. Optional online features (registry contributions, social publishing) are separate.
